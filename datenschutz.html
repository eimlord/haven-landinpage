Was wir verarbeiten

When you use Havn, we process only what's necessary:

  • Your answers to daily questions, and their timestamps
  • Device type and operating system (no fingerprinting)
  • Approximate timezone (no precise location, ever)

We use no cookies. We use no cross-site tracking. We collect no advertising identifiers.

Rechtsgrundlage (Art. 6 DSGVO)

All data processing rests on your explicit consent (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. a DSGVO). You give this when you create an account and accept these terms.

For the internal, anonymous aggregation of usage patterns — used solely to improve the app — we rely on legitimate interest (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. f DSGVO). This aggregation never produces data that could identify you.

Speicherung

All data is stored on servers in Germany, within the EU, subject to GDPR / DSGVO. We use no cloud infrastructure outside the European Economic Area.

We keep your data for as long as you use Havn. After a deletion request, all personal data is fully removed within 30 days. No archives. No backup retention beyond that window.

Weitergabe an Dritte

We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or marketing platforms.

Anonymous, aggregated patterns — never individual answers, never anything traceable to you — may be used internally to understand how the app is working and how to improve it. Nothing leaves our infrastructure in a form that could identify you.

We use no Google Analytics, no Meta Pixel, no third-party analytics of any kind.

Deine Rechte (Art. 15–22 DSGVO)

You have the right to:

  • Access all data we hold about you (Art. 15)
  • Correct inaccurate data (Art. 16)
  • Have your data deleted — "right to be forgotten" (Art. 17)
  • Restrict how we process your data (Art. 18)
  • Receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20)
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21)
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without reason (Art. 7 Abs. 3)

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Austrian data protection authority: dsb.gv.at, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Wien.

Kontakt für Datenschutzanfragen

Write to privacy@havn.app. We respond within 30 days — the legal maximum under DSGVO Art. 12. For complex requests, this may extend to three months; we'll let you know within the first 30 days if that applies.
